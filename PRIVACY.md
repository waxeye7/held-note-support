# Privacy Policy for Held Note

**Effective date: 25 November 2026**

Held Note: Tone Meter ("the app") measures a note you hold on bass or keys. It is made by Merlin Robin Stemmer ("I", "me", the developer). This policy explains what the app does with your data.

## Short version

- No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and I do not sell data.
- Microphone audio is measured in memory and is never recorded or sent anywhere.
- Saved readings stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

## The microphone

The app asks for the microphone once, to measure the note you are playing. The audio is analysed in memory for pitch and tone values and is then discarded. It is never written to a file, never sent to me, and never sent to anyone else.

## What stays on your device

When you tap save, the app stores the reading - the measured values and the time - on your device so you can compare it later (for example before and after a string change). These readings are never sent to me or to anyone else.

## What leaves your device

**Nothing goes to me.** Held Note has no backend, no account system and no analytics, and the app itself makes no network requests.

**App updates.** Updates come through the App Store, which iOS handles itself. The app does not check for updates on its own.

## What the app does not do

The app:

- Has no account or sign-in
- Shows no ads and does not use advertising identifiers
- Does not record or upload microphone audio
- Does not collect your location, contacts or photos
- Does not include analytics or crash reporting
- Does not track you across other apps or websites
- Does not sell or share your data for advertising

Your phone's own backup (iCloud on iPhone) may copy the app's saved readings. That is handled by Apple under its terms, not by me.

## Retention and deletion

Saved readings live only on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. I hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing on my side to delete. If you have a question about your data, email the contact below.
