## Common questions

**It says it cannot hear a clean note.** Play one steady note, closer to the microphone, with less background noise. It refuses to guess when the signal is not clean enough.

**What does it measure?** Pitch to a few cents, where the note sits on a bass neck or keyboard, and four tone values: brightness, harmonic falloff, attack and decay.

**Does it need the internet?** No. Everything is measured on your device from the microphone. Nothing is uploaded.

**Does it keep my audio?** No. Microphone audio is measured in memory and never written or sent. A reading is only kept when you tap save, and saved readings stay on your phone.
